Line follower sensor

November 2024

Technical Analysis

This project involved analysing the structural schematics for a line-following sensor board integrated with an Arduino. The primary challenge was ensuring precise ground-shading detection (black or white) through adjustable potentiometer sensitivity.

1. Preparatory Phase & Dimensioning

The initial objective was to validate the theoretical circuit viability prior to assembly:

  • Theoretical Calculations: Determined resistor values and voltage divider ratios to guarantee the precise operating point of each component.
  • Quality Control: A thorough inspection of the bare PCB identified two critical manufacturing defects—a short-circuit on a LED footprint and a discontinuity on a power trace—preventing immediate failure upon power-up.

2. Technical Challenges & Problem Solving

  • Trace Repair: Performed manual bridging to fix the PCB discontinuity. Critical Analysis: While the connection was functional, this highlighted the need for copper wire jumping (shunting) rather than solder alone to ensure superior conductivity and thermal resistance.
  • SMD Soldering: Mastered solder paste dosage techniques to prevent excess solder and ensure the long-term reliability of Surface Mount Devices.

3. Key Takeaways & Professional Growth

  • Validated Skills: PCB fault diagnosis, complex schematic interpretation, and precision SMD soldering.
  • Future Optimizations: Improving thermal management during soldering to prevent oxidation and refining material application for industrial-grade prototyping.
