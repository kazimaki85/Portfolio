About Me

I am a student in electronic engineering. I'm passionated by electronics, computers, mechanics and 3D modeling. I always want to find out how and therfore why things work. I'm fond of trying to understand all the engineering that have been needed to make something usable and useful.

I am currently studying at the University of Angers where I am learning about electronics and programming.

My Skills

Electronics

During my studies, I learned to fully design, conceive and implemente circuit boards folowing differents standards and rules.

And with that, I learned how to write tests procedures to verify the integrity of my system and to troubleshoot efficiently when there were an issue, I also acquired the capacity to write reports and product documention to explain the design choices I made, the components I used, the results of the tests I performed, how the PCB work and how to properly use it. In the institute of technology and during my Erasmus semester, I learned to use many software such as : Proteus, Kicad and Eagle Eagle for CAD, Ltspice and Orcad for simulation and Labview to create test-benches.

Networking

I acquired a lot of knowledge and knowhow when I was studying in the University Polytehnica de Timisoara, Romania. Teachers like Mr.Savu-Jivanov (CEO and Co-founder of Savnet Training Center) really made me want to learn more about the art of networking.

There, I learned how to set a network, ensuring security, quality of service, scalability and reliability. I used tools such as Wireshark and Cisco Packet Tracer and worked on Cisco hardware. At the and of my study abroad, I obtained the Cisco CCNA certification.

Programming

In highschool, I was teached about Python, C++ and a bit of HTML and CSS. I also learned how to use Arduino and Microbit board to process data and control electronic systems.

During my studies, I learned C language. With it, I have been led to program microcontrollers like the ATmega32 or PIC16 in various situations.

3D Modelling

When I discovered mechanics in highschool, as a Science and Technology of Industry and Sustainable Development student, I was asked to build some projects involving force calculations, movement studies, material selection involving force calculations, movement studies, the choice of materials as well as reflections on the different manufacturing processes that could be used.

Naturally, I started to model every parts of my projects, 3D printing some from time to time. After that, I also learned to simulate movement, Cycle of Life Impact, Manufacturing processes for my mechanical parts on softwares like Solidworks.
